Issue No. 14 — Interiors

Rooms that hold
their quiet.

Atelier Lune designs homes, boutique hotels and private clubs across Europe — led by principal designer Margaux Lune.

M

argaux Lune founded the studio in 2015 after a decade designing hospitality interiors across Paris and Milan. Her approach favours restraint: plaster over paint, reclaimed stone over new, and never more than three materials in a single room.

The studio now leads a team of eleven, working on residential and boutique hospitality projects across eight countries — each one built around what she calls "the discipline of leaving things out."

"A room finishes itself. Our job is to know when to stop."
