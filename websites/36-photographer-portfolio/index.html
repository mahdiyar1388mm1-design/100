About Mara

Mara Voss is a documentary and portrait photographer based in Berlin, with work published in six countries. Her practice centers on unposed, natural-light portraiture — she shoots exclusively on film for personal work.

Available for editorial assignments, brand campaigns, and private portrait sessions worldwide.
