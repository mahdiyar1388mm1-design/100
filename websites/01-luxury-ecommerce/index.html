Founded in 2011 by artisan Elena Vela, Maison Vela produces fewer than 4,000 pieces a year. Every bag, wallet and travel case is cut, skived and hand-stitched by a single craftsperson from start to finish — a practice we call one hand, one object.

Ninety-two hours,
one satchel.

The Aveline Satchel alone requires ninety-two hours of hand labour — from vegetable-tanned hide selection to the final beeswax polish. We believe slowness is a form of respect for the wearer.
